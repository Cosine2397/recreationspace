About Us

Welcome to Recreation Space where coffee, cakes, and creativity come together. Since 1980, our coffee shop and bakery have been serving rich, aromatic coffee and freshly baked treats to our community. Over the years, we've grown from a cozy neighborhood Cafe into a vibrant online space where tradition meets innovation. At Recreation Space, we're more than just a coffee shop or bakery. Here, you can order your favorite coffee and cakes anytime, play fun games and quizzes to earn discounts, and even share your creativity by adding photos and personal notes to our community gallery. For coffee lovers looking to learn and explore, our interactive flashcard quizzes make discovering coffee fun and rewarding. For over four decades, we've been committed to blending great taste with great experiences. Now, we invite you to sip, savor, play, and create with us because at Recreation Space, this space is yours, too.
